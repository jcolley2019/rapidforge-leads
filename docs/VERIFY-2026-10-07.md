## 6. Open issues

- **V1 (high): Builder Brief is billed twice and races a 60 s timeout.** `builder-brief.ts:186` sets `maxTokens: 4000`. On Opus 4.8 the thinking tokens count against it, so attempt 1 always stops at `max_tokens` and `generateMarkdown` (lib/ai.ts:620) retries at 8,000. Token counts show this: All Plumbing's 8,196 = one ~4.2k-in / 4,000-out attempt; the successes are 16.7k = two attempts. The worker passes no `timeoutMs`, so AI Core's `DEFAULT_TIMEOUT_MS = 60000` applies (ai-core 0.9.0 `providers/provider.js:3`, no retry on timeout). Attempt 1 takes about 55 s, so the doubled retry has to finish inside that 60 s request timeout; 1 of 3 did not, and it stored the template after spending 12¢. Successes cost 24¢ (doc 11.4¢). The total of 109–115 s sits just under the 120 s on-demand budget (orchestrator.ts:72). Fix direction: raise the first-attempt cap so one call finishes, and pass a `timeoutMs` that fits the 120 s budget.
- **V2 (high): screenshots fail on `http://` sites.** The bundled Chrome 150 (`win64-150.0.7871.24`) returns `net::ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT` for `http://www.accurbore.com/`, which is Chrome's HTTPS-upgrade / HTTPS-First handling. Reproduced outside the worker: the same binary with `--disable-features=HttpsUpgrades,HttpsFirstBalancedModeAutoEnable,HttpsFirstModeV2ForEngagedSites` loads it (HTTP 200). `LAUNCH_ARGS` is lib/browser.ts:43. These are exactly the legacy no-SSL pages that most need the vision Design pass. The internal error also reaches the rep-facing issue list and the Analyst prompt as "Screenshot unavailable (net::ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT …)" (scorer.ts:223).
- **V3 (medium, Joey's call; protected `scoring.ts`): with `PSI_DESKTOP=true` the desktop score now carries the performance term.** Landers went 58 · 3★ · 68 → 72 · 4★ · 55 (`healthy_site` cap) with mobile 48 and LCP 13.6 s, and lost its Analyst run. All Plumbing went 72 → 78. Option (a) from AUDIT "Needs Joey's call #1" fixed Accurbore-type misses but flipped Landers the other way. Weights or the cap rule need a look before more real audits.
- **V4 (medium): Health narration contradicts the audit on legacy pages.** Accurbore's Health one-liner says "Site is healthy" and "Detected platform: custom". Health runs before the Scorer's `legacy_static` override, and its template keys on PSI alone. A rep reads "healthy" next to a 3★ rebuild verdict.
- **V5 (low): `audits.completed_at` is the pipeline start time.** orchestrator.ts:311 captures `now` once and scorer.ts:362 writes it, so all three audits show `completed_at` ≈ `created_at` + 2 s while the jobs finished 34–54 s later and the Analyst ran after "completion". Wall time has to come from `jobs.finished_at`.
- **V6 (low): Web: the Leads list, palette and drawer stay on the old audit after a re-audit until a page reload.** Landers kept showing health 58 / sell 68 and "Audited 4d ago" after its new 72 / 55 audit finished. After a reload all views were correct. Each business with three `search_results` rows also appears three times in the command palette.
- **V7 (low): one fact, three carriers.** The money-facts JSON carries "no viewport" as design.critical_issues, `conversion.has_viewport_meta` and an issue, and the Analyst repeats it under two agents (row 16).
- **V8 (low): Builder Brief regenerates the Design Brief even when one is stored.** All Plumbing's brief embeds a fresh Haiku Design Brief that differs from the stored `audits.design_brief`, which is kept. This is a wasted call, and the brief text and the Design Brief tab disagree.
- **V9 (low): the boot log misreports `AI_SUMMARIES`.** index.ts:42 calls `narrationSummaryMode()` without an agent, so with `AI_SUMMARIES=reputation` it prints "narration summaries …: template (set AI_SUMMARIES=haiku…)". The runs did use Haiku for Reputation.
- **V10 (low): stale copy.** The Builder Brief tab says "Generate a paste-ready rebuild brief (Fable 5)" (apps/web/src/components/leads/LeadDrawer.tsx:1095; header comment :991), but the agent runs on Opus 4.8.
