## Findings

### P0 — broken or exposed now

**P0-1. Anyone can sign up and spend on your keys.**
- Evidence: probe 2.12 `disable_signup=false … providers=google,email`. `apps/web/src/features/auth/useAuth.ts:81` calls `bootstrap_workspace` for any signed-in user with no membership; `supabase/migrations/0004_rls.sql:166-167` gives that workspace the `founder` plan with `monthly_search_limit null`. `apps/worker/src/middleware/auth.ts:80-87` accepts any user that has a workspace. No rate limit or ceiling exists (P1-1).
- Impact: one search can cost about $5 of Places (1 geocode + up to 48 tiles × 3¢ + up to 100 details × 4¢; `apps/worker/src/lib/geo.ts:37`, `apps/worker/src/agents/scout.ts:34`) plus up to 100 audits of Sonnet and Haiku, and it can be looped. Not yet abused: probe 2.13 shows 1 user.
- Fix — **RFL.AUTH.1**, S. Today, no code: Supabase dashboard → Authentication → turn off "Allow new users to sign up". Then in `middleware/auth.ts` add an `ALLOWED_USER_IDS` / `ALLOWED_EMAILS` env check after `getUser` (403 otherwise) and a test; document it in `docs/DEPLOY.md`.

**P0-2. One signed-in request crashes the worker.**
- Evidence: `apps/worker/src/http.ts:574` `const ref = decodeURIComponent(req.params.ref);` runs before the handler's `try`, in an async Express 4.22.2 handler; `apps/worker/src` has no `unhandledRejection` handler. Repro (same handler shape, local): `GET /api/places/photo/%25` → `URIError: URI malformed` → `process exit code 1`.
- Impact: the process dies, every in-flight audit is orphaned for 10 minutes (P1-2), Railway restarts it; repeatable in a loop. Needs a valid JWT, which P0-1 hands to anyone.
- Fix — **RFL.HTTP.1**, S. Move the decode inside the `try` and answer 400; add `process.on("unhandledRejection")` logging in `index.ts`; a test for `%25`.

**P0-3. A member can write jobs, businesses and audits directly, and the worker trusts them (SSRF, cross-workspace jobs).**
- Evidence: `0004_rls.sql:93-95, 107-109, 114-116, 121-123, 128-130` grant `authenticated` INSERT on `businesses`, `audits`, `jobs`, `agent_runs`, `usage_events` for their own workspace — reachable with the public anon key plus a session. `apps/worker/src/orchestrator.ts:238-243` loads the job's search and business and never compares their `workspace_id` to each other or to the job's. No private-address check exists in `lib/site.ts`, `lib/probe.ts`, `lib/screenshots.ts` or `lib/browser.ts` (grep for loopback, link-local or DNS checks: none); Chrome runs `--no-sandbox` (`lib/browser.ts:53`), the image has no `USER` line (`Dockerfile`), and hosted capture is on (`screenshot_capture_mode=real`).
- Impact: insert a business whose `website_url` is an internal address, then `POST /api/businesses/:id/reaudit` (`http.ts:635`) or insert the job row; the response comes back through `audits.http_status`, the SEO title, the brief excerpt and a screenshot in the public bucket. A forged job can also name another workspace's business — ids are enumerable by anyone (probe 2.11).
- Fix — **RFL.RLS.1**, M. Migration 0012: drop the member INSERT policies on `businesses`, `audits`, `jobs`, `agent_runs`, `usage_events`, `search_results`, `searches` (the web writes none of them; it goes through the worker). In `handleAuditBusinessJob` and `handleScoutJob` fail the job when workspaces differ. **RFL.SSRF.1**, M: one `assertPublicUrl` (resolve, reject loopback / private / link-local, re-check each redirect) used by site, probe and screenshot.

### P1 — will bite

**P1-1. No rate limit and no spend ceiling.** `monthly_search_limit`, `monthly_audit_limit` and `max_radius_miles` appear only in `packages/shared/src/schemas.ts:173-175`, never in `apps/worker`. `AUDIT_CONCURRENCY_CAP` is global, and the budgets in `lib/budget.ts` are time only. The <$200 target is a convention, not a check; a stuck retry loop in the web app would be enough. — **RFL.SPEND.1**, M: in `POST /api/searches`, `reaudit` and the four on-demand AI routes, sum month-to-date `usage_events` for the workspace and answer 429 past a `MONTHLY_CENTS_CEILING` env (default 20000); small per-workspace token bucket on the same routes.

**P1-2. No SIGTERM handling — every redeploy orphans running jobs for 10 minutes.** `apps/worker/src/index.ts` registers no signal handler and the image starts through npm (`Dockerfile` `CMD ["npm","run","start",…]`). Recovery is the reaper only: `apps/worker/src/queue.ts:33` `STALE_AFTER_MS = 10 * 60_000`, then requeue while `attempts < MAX_JOB_ATTEMPTS` (`queue.ts:30`, 2). A scout killed mid-run pays Places again; a job already on attempt 2 fails. — **RFL.HOST.8**, S: on SIGTERM stop the poller, abort in-flight jobs and requeue them without burning an attempt; start with `npx tsx` directly so the signal reaches Node.

**P1-3. Cancel and clear-all do not stop a running scout; clear-all has no active-search check.** `orchestrator.ts:163` `return handleScoutJob(job, deps);` passes no abort signal, so after a cancel the scout still enqueues its audits and sets the search back to `auditing`. `http.ts:429-452` calls `clearWorkspaceData` with no `isSearchActive` check and no abort, unlike delete-search (`http.ts:399`): in-flight agents finish their paid calls, their writes update zero rows, later `agent_runs` inserts fail on the job FK, screenshots upload after the clear. No crash and no stuck poller was found by reading. — **RFL.QUEUE.9**, M: thread the signal into `handleScoutJob` and re-check search status before fan-out; clear-all cancels every active search first (or answers 409).

**P1-4. Stage budgets add up past the audit ceiling, so a slow Analyst re-runs the whole audit.** `orchestrator.ts:77-85`: probe 15 + fetch 15 + psi 45 + screenshot 30 + agent 60 + analyst 120 s against `AUDIT_CEILING_MS = 4 * 60_000`. The ceiling fires after scoring is done; the queue requeues, and PSI and every AI call are paid twice. `orchestrator.ts:747` rethrows `StageAbortedError` before the run row is closed, leaving it `running` until the reaper. — **RFL.QUEUE.10**, S: give the Analyst `min(120 s, time left)`, close the row before rethrowing, test with a slow fake.

**P1-5. An audit with no PSI measurement is cached for 30 days.** `orchestrator.ts:249-255` skips the cache only for `provisional`; a PSI failure sets `psi_unmeasured` (`agents/scorer.ts:267`) but `orchestrator.ts:648` hard-codes `provisional: false`. A 429 from PSI gives a neutral-scored lead that is then a cache hit for a month and can trigger the Analyst. — **RFL.PSI.2**, S: treat `score_breakdown.psi_unmeasured` as not cacheable and exclude it from the Analyst auto-run gate.

**P1-6. Hosted AI is unproven and Settings are empty.** No `agent_runs` row since 2026-10-26 and nothing identifies the writing worker, so this audit cannot say the Railway Anthropic key works (`/health` `ai_mode=core` only means a key is present). `workspace_config` has no offer, location, industry or traits, so every Analyst, Builder Brief and Sales Summary runs on fallback wording. — Joey: fill Settings, then run one small hosted search and one brief. **RFL.AI.11**, S: stamp `input.host` (Railway deployment id or `local`) on every `agent_runs` row and add `last_ai_ok_at` / `last_ai_error` to `/health`.

**P1-7. Vulnerable runtime dependencies on a public server.** `npm audit --omit=dev`: 7 high, 1 critical. Relevant to the worker: `body-parser 1.20.5` (high, DoS — GHSA-v422-hmwv-36x6, GHSA-27r3-jxmh-p828), `zod 4.6.5` (high, memory exhaustion on oversized collections — GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5; the 100 kb JSON limit softens it), `puppeteer-core` via `chromium-bidi` → zod. `proxy-addr 2.0.7` (critical, GHSA-jqcg-44mw-7w3h) only matters with `trust proxy`, which is not set. `postcss`, `nanoid`, `source-map-js` are build-time. — **RFL.DEPS.1**, S: `npm audit fix` (non-breaking for all but zod), bump worker zod to ≥ 4.6.6, re-run the three suites.

**P1-8. Reputation narration fails its guardrail 6 times in 13.** Data health above: Haiku paraphrases or over-quotes review text and fails "verbatim" / "too long" twice in a row; the flagged summary is stored and shown. — **RFL.REP.1**, S: have the prompt return review index + character span and let the worker cut the quote, or fall back to the deterministic template on a second failure.
